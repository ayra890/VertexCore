Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about VPS plans, billing and support.

What is a Windows VPS?
A Windows VPS is a virtual server running a Windows operating system that you can access remotely through RDP.
How do I get started?
Choose a VPS plan, complete the order form and follow the account and deployment instructions provided by Vertex Core.
Can I upgrade my plan later?
Plans can be structured to support scaling as your resource requirements grow.
What payment methods do you accept?
The order interface can support card, JazzCash and Easypaisa options.
How do I receive my VPS credentials?
After successful provisioning, your VPS IP address and access credentials can be provided through your customer dashboard and email.
How can I contact support?
