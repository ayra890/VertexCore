Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Vertex Core may collect, use and protect information when you use our website and services.

Vertex Core respects the privacy of customers and website visitors. This policy describes the types of information that may be collected and how that information may be used in connection with our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide when creating an account, placing an order, contacting support or using our services. This may include your name, email address, contact details, billing information and service-related information.

Account Information

Account information is used to create and manage your Vertex Core account, provide requested services and communicate with you about your account.

Payment Information

Payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers. Vertex Core may receive transaction-related information necessary to confirm payments, maintain billing records and provide services. Sensitive payment credentials may be handled directly by the applicable payment provider.

Communications

We may use your contact information to respond to support requests, service notices, account communications, billing notifications and other necessary operational messages.

Technical and Log Information

Our website and infrastructure may collect technical information such as IP address, browser information, device information, access times, diagnostic information and other data needed for security and operation of the service.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide and maintain services, process orders, manage accounts, provide customer support, improve website functionality, prevent abuse, protect infrastructure and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Cookies

Vertex Core may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, remember preferences and understand website usage. Browser settings may allow you to manage or disable certain cookies.

Service Providers

We may use trusted third-party providers for services such as payment processing, hosting infrastructure, communications, analytics, security or other operational requirements. Such providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

Data Security

Vertex Core uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and billing records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or satisfy applicable legal obligations.

Information Sharing

Vertex Core does not intend to sell customer information. Information may be shared with service providers or other parties where necessary to provide services, protect the infrastructure, process transactions, comply with legal requirements or address security concerns.

User Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including requesting access, correction or other available privacy choices. Requests can be directed to the appropriate Vertex Core contact channel.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intentionally directed toward children where such services are not appropriate for them. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

Policy Changes

Vertex Core may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Changes will be published on this page, together with an updated effective date where appropriate.

Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact Vertex Core through the contact information provided on our website.
