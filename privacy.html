01 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Vertex Core ("we," "our," or "us") may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you visit our website or interact with our products and services.

By using our website, you acknowledge this policy. Where consent is legally required, we will request it separately. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the relevant services.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, information may include:

  • Contact information: name, email address, and details you submit through contact or support forms.
  • Account information: account identifiers and authentication-related information, where accounts are provided.
  • Order information: VPS plan, order details, billing records, and transaction status where applicable.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and website diagnostic logs.
  • Support communications: messages, troubleshooting details, and attachments you choose to send us.
  • Usage information: pages visited and interactions with the website, if analytics or similar tools are enabled.

Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other highly sensitive information through ordinary contact forms. Information collected depends on the features actually enabled.

03 How We Use Information

We may use relevant information to:

  • Respond to questions and provide customer support.
  • Process and manage service orders and customer requests.
  • Maintain, operate, and improve our website and services.
  • Detect suspicious activity, prevent abuse, and protect system security.
  • Maintain business records and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Send service-related updates or other communications where permitted.

We will not describe information as being used for a purpose that does not reflect our actual practices. Where required, we will obtain consent before using information for optional purposes such as certain marketing activities.

04 Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, understand website performance, or measure usage.

Essential technologies may be needed for features to work. Non-essential analytics or advertising technologies, if enabled, will be handled according to applicable consent requirements and our actual configuration.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality. If we do not use analytics or advertising cookies, this section should be updated accordingly.

05 Information Sharing

We do not treat personal information as something to sell. We may share relevant information only where appropriate, such as:

  • Service providers: hosting infrastructure, email delivery, customer support, security, or payment providers we actually use.
  • Legal requirements: where disclosure is required by applicable law or valid legal process.
  • Security and protection: where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect rights, or safeguard our services and users.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of business, subject to applicable law.

The information shared will depend on the situation and the service involved. We do not claim to use any particular provider unless that provider is actually part of our setup.

06 Data Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to our operations to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

No website, internet transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use strong, unique passwords where accounts are available and notify us if you suspect unauthorized use of your account.

07 Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing services, resolving disputes, maintaining records, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it was collected. Information that is no longer required should be securely deleted or anonymized where appropriate and technically feasible.

08 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict particular processing activities.

You may also have rights concerning consent withdrawal, data portability, or complaints to a relevant privacy authority where provided by law.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before responding. Some requests may be subject to lawful exceptions.

09 Third-Party Services & Links

Our website may link to third-party websites or use external services. Those parties may have their own privacy policies and data handling practices.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party websites. Please review their policies before providing information to them.

10 Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Where parental consent or additional protections are legally required, those requirements must be followed.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Where required by law, we will provide notice of material changes or obtain consent before applying new practices. Please review this page periodically.
