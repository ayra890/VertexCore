🔎 Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Vertex Core ("we," "our," or "us") may collect, use, store, and disclose information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

Where applicable law requires consent for particular processing activities, we will request that consent separately. Please review this policy to understand the practices relevant to your use of our website.

ℹ Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Contact details: Your name, email address, and information submitted through contact or support forms.
  • Order information: Selected plans, order details, and relevant billing records, where applicable.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, device information, timestamps, and diagnostic logs.
  • Support communications: Messages and troubleshooting information you choose to provide.
  • Account information: Account identifiers and related details if account functionality is provided.
  • Usage information: Website visits and interactions if analytics tools are enabled.

The information collected depends on the services and features actually enabled. Please avoid submitting passwords or payment card details through ordinary contact forms.

📇 How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to inquiries and providing customer support.
  • Managing service requests and orders.
  • Operating, maintaining, and improving the website.
  • Detecting suspicious activity and protecting service security.
  • Maintaining business records and meeting legal obligations.
  • Sending relevant service communications where permitted.

We aim to use information for legitimate, disclosed purposes and to follow applicable privacy requirements.

📊 Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential functionality, remember preferences, or understand website performance.

If non-essential analytics or advertising technologies are used, we will handle them according to applicable consent requirements. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features. This section should be adjusted to match the tools actually installed on the website.

📢 Information Sharing

We may share relevant information where reasonably necessary for providing services, operating the website, or meeting legal obligations.

  • Service providers: Providers supporting hosting, email, security, customer support, or payment processing, if used.

We do not sell personal information as a business practice. Any sharing will depend on the actual service and applicable legal requirements.

🔐 Data Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards appropriate to our operations to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, please contact us through our support channel.

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, respond to requests, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected. Information that is no longer required should be deleted or anonymized where appropriate and feasible.
