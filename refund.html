Refund Policy

Please review the following refund and cancellation terms before purchasing Vertex Core services.

This Refund Policy explains the general process used by Vertex Core for cancellation and refund requests related to VPS and associated services.

Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on the service purchased, the reason for the request, the time since purchase and any applicable exclusions. A refund is not automatically guaranteed for every cancellation.

Refund Requests

Customers requesting a refund should contact Vertex Core through the available support or contact channel and provide the account and service information necessary to identify the transaction.

Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of a service. Cancellation stops future service obligations according to the applicable billing cycle, but does not automatically create a refund for previously consumed service time.

Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds may not be available in situations such as policy violations, abuse of services, chargeback-related disputes, services already substantially consumed, or other circumstances where a refund is excluded by the applicable service terms.

Service Usage

Customers should review their VPS configuration, plan requirements and service details before completing an order. Usage of a service may affect eligibility for cancellation or refund.

Payment Processing

Approved refunds may be processed through the original payment method where supported. Processing time can vary depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

Payment Disputes & Chargebacks

If you believe a payment was made incorrectly, please contact Vertex Core before initiating a payment dispute or chargeback. This allows the billing issue to be reviewed and resolved where appropriate.

Promotional Offers

Promotional pricing, credits, discounts or special offers may have specific conditions. Such conditions may affect refund eligibility and will apply according to the terms presented with the promotion.

Abuse or Policy Violations

Refund requests associated with prohibited activities, abuse, fraudulent behavior or serious violations of the Terms of Services may be declined.

Refund Review

Vertex Core may review the account, payment and service history before making a refund decision. Additional information may be requested when necessary to verify the request.

Exceptions

In exceptional circumstances, Vertex Core may review an individual case separately. Any exception provided for one transaction does not automatically create a general entitlement to the same exception for future transactions.

Contact

For refund or cancellation questions, please contact Vertex Core through the website contact page.
